Content built for the moment a skincare question becomes a search.

I translated that persona’s questions into a keyword strategy, then into three articles built to inform, guide and build trust.

03 — Target audience

One persona, defined by how he searches.

High-intent segment

Carlos

The Practical Skincare Researcher

A 36-year-old Latino man who uses Google to solve skincare problems like dryness and product confusion. He represents a high-intent, underserved audience that prefers functional, science-backed solutions.

Pain points

Dry skin and confusion about which products to use

Priorities

Functional, science-backed information

Search habits

Searches to educate himself, compare options and find simple routines

Channel fit

Not active on Instagram or TikTok, so search and YouTube are the channels that reach him

Why it matters: the channel choice followed the audience’s behavior, not the other way around. For Carlos, search is the primary route to the brand.

05 — Content strategy

I selected keywords from the research and developed three SEO-focused blog posts designed to inform, guide and build trust, each matched to a different type of search.

Article 01 Informational

Simple Skincare Routine for Men: A No-Stress Guide for Dry Skin

Makes the starting point feel simple, with a routine built around dryness and beginner questions.

Keywords worked in

  • simple skincare routine for men
  • why is my skin so dry even after moisturizing
  • how often should men wash face
Read the full draft

If you’ve ever Googled “simple skincare routine for men” and felt confused, you’re not alone. A lot of guys just want something that works without overcomplicating things.

The truth is, you don’t need 10 products. You just need a few basics that actually help your skin—especially if you’re dealing with dryness.

Step 1: Start with the Right Cleanser

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make is using harsh soap. It might feel clean, but it strips your skin and makes dryness worse.

If you’re dealing with dry skin, look for something gentle like a hydrating cleanser. Products like CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser are designed to clean your face without removing your natural moisture barrier.

This matters because your skin barrier is what keeps hydration in.

Step 2: Moisturize (Yes, Every Day)

A lot of people search “why is my skin so dry even after moisturizing.” Usually, it’s because they’re either skipping moisturizer or using the wrong one.

A good fragrance-free moisturizer helps restore your skin instead of irritating it. This is especially important if you have sensitive skin.

Look for ingredients like ceramides and hyaluronic acid—they help your skin hold onto water.

Step 3: Keep It Consistent

You don’t need a complicated routine. Just:

  • Wash your face twice a day
  • Use moisturizer after

That’s it.

If you’re wondering how often men should wash their face, twice a day is enough. Overwashing can actually make dryness worse.

Why This Routine Works

This routine focuses on repairing your skin barrier instead of overwhelming it. That’s exactly what most beginners need.

If you’re just starting out, simple is better. Once your skin improves, you can always add more steps later.

CeraVe vs Cetaphil Cleanser: Which One Is Better for Dry Skin?

Addresses product confusion with comparison-led content for someone choosing between two options.

If you’ve been comparing “CeraVe vs Cetaphil cleanser,” you’re probably trying to figure out which one actually works better.

Both brands are popular for a reason. But they’re not exactly the same.

What They Have in Common

Both are:

  • Fragrance-free
  • Designed for sensitive skin
  • Affordable (drugstore options)

That’s why they show up so often when people search for the best skincare products for men.

Where They’re Different

CeraVe focuses more on restoring your skin barrier. Its cleansers include ceramides, which help repair and protect your skin.

Cetaphil is known for being gentle, but it doesn’t focus as much on rebuilding the skin barrier.

If your main issue is dryness or flaky skin, this difference actually matters.

Which One Should You Choose?

If you’re dealing with:

  • Dry skin
  • Flaky patches
  • Tight feeling after washing

CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser is usually the better choice.

It cleans your face without making dryness worse, which is something a lot of guys struggle with.

What Causes Flaky Skin Anyway?

A lot of people search “what causes flaky skin on face.”

Usually it comes down to:

  • Overwashing
  • Using harsh products
  • Not moisturizing properly

That’s why switching to a gentler cleanser can make a big difference.

Final Thought

If you want something simple, safe, and effective, both brands work.

But if your goal is fixing dryness long-term, CeraVe is designed more for that.

Article 03 Informational

How to Pick the Right Cleanser (Without Wasting Money)

Turns an overwhelming product choice into a practical guide to skin type and cleanser selection.

Keywords worked in

  • how to pick the right cleanser
  • fragrance free skincare for sensitive skin
  • difference between cleanser and face wash
Read the full draft

Choosing a face wash shouldn’t feel complicated, but it does for a lot of people.

If you’ve searched “how to pick the right cleanser,” you’ve probably seen too many options with no clear answer.

Here’s how to actually figure it out.

Step 1: Know Your Skin Type

Before anything else, figure out if your skin is:

  • Dry
  • Oily
  • Combination

If your skin feels tight or rough, it’s probably dry.

That’s important because using the wrong cleanser can make things worse.

Step 2: Avoid Harsh Ingredients

A lot of face washes are too aggressive. They remove oil, but they also damage your skin barrier.

That’s why fragrance-free skincare for sensitive skin is usually the safer option.

It reduces irritation and keeps things simple.

Step 3: Look for Barrier-Friendly Ingredients

If you’re choosing between products, look for ingredients like:

  • Ceramides
  • Hyaluronic acid

These help your skin stay hydrated instead of drying out.

This is why products like CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser are popular—they’re designed to support your skin, not strip it.

Step 4: Don’t Overcomplicate It

You don’t need multiple cleansers.

A simple routine works best:

  • One gentle cleanser
  • One moisturizer

That’s it.

Cleanser vs Face Wash (Quick Tip)

If you’ve ever wondered about the difference between cleanser and face wash:

  • Face wash = usually foaming, stronger
  • Cleanser = gentler, better for dry skin

If your skin is dry, go with a cleanser.

06 — AI & SEO learning

Search is changing. The reader’s need still matters.

The project also explored how Google AI Overviews are changing SEO, and what that means for how blog content should be written.

01

Answer clearly

Address specific questions directly and concisely.

02

Use strong headings

Clear headings and short explanations are easy for AI to pull summaries from.

03

Stay close to intent

Solve the problem behind the search, not just the keyword.

04

Add depth

Give readers value beyond what an AI summary can cover, so they have a reason to click.

“I would balance being informative upfront while still providing value in the full article.” My reflection on writing for AI Overviews
